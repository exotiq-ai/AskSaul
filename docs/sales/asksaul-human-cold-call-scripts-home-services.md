# AskSaul.ai Human Cold Call Scripts — Home Services First Batch

**Audience:** Human callers prospecting construction, roofing, renovations, home remodeling, restoration, trades, and related local-service companies.  
**Goal:** Start a real conversation, identify missed-call / slow-follow-up revenue leaks, and book a short Saul demo or callback with Gregory.  
**Primary CTA:** Call the Saul demo line: **+1 (970) 401-7285** or book a **15-minute walkthrough with Gregory**.  
**Positioning:** Saul is a revenue-protection layer, not generic AI phone software.

---

## 1. Core positioning for callers

### Plain-English pitch

AskSaul.ai helps local service businesses stop losing jobs from missed calls, slow follow-up, and inconsistent lead handling. Saul is an AI voice agent that answers calls, qualifies leads, captures the details, and gets the next step into the team’s workflow.

### What Saul does

- Answers inbound calls when the team is busy, closed, on a jobsite, or already on another call.
- Captures name, phone number, service need, location, timing, and urgency.
- Qualifies the caller using the company’s rules.
- Handles simple FAQs and routes complex calls back to the team.
- Creates clean call summaries and callback notes.
- Can feed contacts, notes, opportunities, tags, and follow-up into GoHighLevel / LeadConnector workflows.
- Helps owners wake up to qualified opportunities instead of mystery voicemails.

### Best framing for contractors and home-service owners

Most of these businesses do not need “more AI.” They need fewer missed opportunities.

Use this frame:

> You are already paying to make the phone ring through referrals, Google, ads, yard signs, trucks, SEO, repeat customers, and reputation. Saul helps make sure more of those calls actually get answered, captured, and followed up with before the customer calls the next contractor.

---

## 2. Caller rules before dialing

1. **Sound like a person, not a pitch deck.** Short sentences. Natural pauses. Do not overexplain AI.
2. **Ask for permission early.** Owners get hammered by vendors. Respect time.
3. **Lead with the business leak.** Missed calls, after-hours calls, busy crews, slow callbacks, unlogged leads.
4. **Do not claim guaranteed revenue.** Use simple math and “if” language.
5. **Do not argue.** If they say no, exit cleanly.
6. **Get to a live demo fast.** Hearing Saul is stronger than describing Saul.
7. **Position Saul as support, not replacement.** He covers gaps humans miss.
8. **For roofing / construction / remodelers:** connect the pain to quote requests, storm calls, insurance questions, project timelines, after-hours homeowners, and crews being in the field.

---

## 3. Quick call structure

Use this structure for almost every cold call:

1. **Opener:** Who you are + why you are calling.
2. **Permission:** “Can I take 20 seconds?”
3. **Problem hypothesis:** Missed calls / slow follow-up / busy crews.
4. **Simple Saul bridge:** Saul answers, captures, qualifies, and hands off.
5. **Question:** Confirm whether the pain exists.
6. **CTA:** Demo line or 15-minute walkthrough.

# 7. Discovery questions callers can use

Use only 2–4 on the cold call. Save the rest for a booked demo.

1. How many inbound calls do you get in a normal week?
2. What happens when a call comes in after hours?
3. Who answers when your team is on a jobsite or already on another call?
4. How many calls go to voicemail or get missed each week?
5. What is an average booked job worth for you?
6. How fast do quote requests usually get called back?
7. Are calls logged anywhere, or do they live in phones, texts, sticky notes, and memory?
8. Do you use GoHighLevel, another CRM, or mostly phone/email?
9. What calls should be escalated to a human immediately?
10. If a safe first version could go live in 1–2 weeks, who would need to approve it?

# 8. ROI talk track

Do not overpromise. Use simple, believable math.

### Formula

```text
Missed qualified calls × close rate × average job value = possible monthly leakage
```

### Example for home services

```text
If 20 qualified calls a month are missed or followed up too late, and you close 25% of those at a $1,500 average job value, that is $7,500/month in possible leakage.

Saul does not need to win every call. He just needs to save enough of the calls you are already losing.
```

### Human-cost comparison

```text
A fully loaded phone hire can easily run $4,000–$7,500/month before you count nights, weekends, vacations, sick days, and multiple calls at once. Saul is not a replacement for your best human. He is the coverage layer that catches what humans miss.
```

# 12. Recommended first-batch dialing plan

### Lead segments

Tag or mentally group leads before calling:

- `industry:roofing`
- `industry:construction`
- `industry:remodeling`
- `industry:renovation`
- `industry:restoration`
- `industry:general-contractor`
- `industry:home-services`

### Prioritize first

1. Companies with strong call-driven services: roofing, restoration, emergency repairs.
2. Companies spending on ads or with visible SEO/local-service presence.
3. Owner-operated firms where the owner is likely stretched thin.
4. Remodelers and renovation companies with high average job values.
5. Businesses already using GoHighLevel or similar CRMs.

### Call objective

The objective is **not** to sell the whole system on the cold call. The objective is one of:

- Get them to call Saul’s demo line.
- Book a 15-minute walkthrough with Gregory.
- Identify a real missed-call / after-hours pain.
- Suppress bad-fit or uninterested leads cleanly.
