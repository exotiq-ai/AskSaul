## Recommended design direction

This customer-facing document should use the **light trust/ROI theme** for readability and lower friction with older or tech-cautious owners.

### Light theme: “The Revenue Leak Report”

**Mood:** clean, confident, readable, practical, premium but not intimidating.  
**Audience:** local business owners, operators, office managers, practice owners, service companies, sales leaders.  
**Goal:** make the owner think, “I am losing money every time the phone goes unanswered. I should hear the demo.”

### Visual style

- Background: warm off-white / light paper / soft gray.
- Text: charcoal / deep navy.
- Accent: AskSaul signal cyan `#00D4AA` used sparingly for CTA, chart highlights, checkmarks.
- Secondary accent: ice blue `#4AC6E8` for data cards.
- Avoid purple.
- Use large, readable typography; no tiny SaaS-print.
- Use owner-friendly diagrams: phone rings → Saul answers → lead captured → team follows up.
- Include one simple ROI chart, not a complex dashboard.
- Keep pricing blocks clean and non-scary.

### Optional dark variant

If Claude Design creates a second version, use the dark Saul command-center style:

- Obsidian `#0A0A0F`
- Carbon cards `#12121A`
- Graphite `#1A1A24`
- Cloud text `#E8E8ED`
- Signal Cyan `#00D4AA`
- Ice Blue `#4AC6E8`
- Subtle grid/noise, glass cards, controlled glow.

Dark theme should feel like **precision engineering meets midnight command center**, not generic neon AI.

## Section 3 — ROI chart

# What is one missed call worth?

Saul does not need to replace your whole team to pay for itself. It only needs to catch the opportunities currently slipping through voicemail.

### Revenue leakage examples

Use this as a simple chart in the sales sheet:

- **Home services:** 20 missed qualified calls × 25% close rate × $250–$1,500 job value = **$1,250–$7,500/mo potential leakage**
- **Dental / med spa:** 20 × 25% × $300–$3,000 value = **$1,500–$15,000/mo potential leakage**
- **Legal / consulting:** 20 × 25% × $1,000–$10,000 value = **$5,000–$50,000/mo potential leakage**
- **Dumpster / waste:** 20 × 25% × $350–$2,000 value = **$1,750–$10,000/mo potential leakage**

### Human cost comparison

A fully loaded phone employee can easily cost:

- **$3,900/mo** at $18/hr fully loaded
- **$4,767/mo** at $22/hr fully loaded
- **$6,067/mo** at $28/hr fully loaded
- **$7,583/mo** at $35/hr fully loaded

And one human still cannot reliably cover every night, weekend, overflow moment, sick day, vacation, or simultaneous call.

**ROI line:**

> If Saul helps recover just a handful of qualified calls each month, the system can pay for itself before you count the time your team gets back.
