- “Saul covers the phones, captures the opportunity, and hands your team the next move.”
- “Your team should wake up to qualified opportunities, not mystery voicemails.”

## 4. Fast discovery questions

Ask 2–3 max on a cold call.

1. “What happens when a call comes in after hours?”
2. “Who answers when your team is on a jobsite or already on another call?”
3. “How many calls go to voicemail or get missed in a normal week?”
4. “What is an average booked job worth for you?”
5. “Do missed calls get logged anywhere, or are they mostly in phones and voicemail?”
6. “Are you using GoHighLevel or another CRM today?”

```text
The math is pretty simple. Missed qualified calls times close rate times average job value equals possible monthly leakage.

So if even 10–20 decent calls a month are missed or followed up too late, and a few of those would have turned into booked jobs, Saul only needs to save a handful to make the system worth looking at.
```

Would you be more comfortable starting with after-hours and missed-call capture only?

# 11. Do / do not

## Do

- Ask permission early.
- Keep it human and short.
- Lead with missed jobs, not AI.
- Push toward the demo line quickly.
- Frame Saul as overflow / after-hours / missed-call support.
- Ask what one booked job is worth.
- Exit politely when they say no.

## Do not

- Lead with technical jargon.
- Say Saul replaces the receptionist.
- Promise guaranteed revenue.
- Argue with AI skepticism.
- Ask for 30 minutes on a cold call.
- Overexplain all features.
- Keep contacting opt-outs.
